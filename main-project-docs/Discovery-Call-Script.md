# AgentOps Studio — Discovery Call Script (MKT-07)

> **Version history**
> - [2026-10-12 17:30] v1.0 — CEO Agent — First version (MKT-07, `gaps.md` § 17). Niche-neutral; electrical/hardware retail used as the example until D2 is decided. Feeds GTM-03 (30-day plan) and the client delivery SOP (OFR-02).

**Goal of the call:** find out whether missed or badly handled calls cost this business real money, and agree a next step. Not to pitch features.
**Length:** 20–25 minutes. You talk ≤ 30% of the time.
**Before the call:** call the business once after hours and once at a busy time (e.g. 12–1 pm). Note whether anyone answered, how long it rang, and what happened. This is your opening fact.

---

## 1. Open (2 min)

> "Thanks for making the time. I'll ask a few questions about how calls work at [business] today. If it looks like we can help, I'll show you how; if not, I'll tell you that too. Is that okay?"

## 2. Current situation (5 min)

1. Roughly how many calls do you get on a normal day? On your busiest day?
2. Who answers them? What happens when that person is busy with a customer at the counter?
3. What happens to a call that comes in at 8 pm, or on a Sunday?
4. *(Use your test call)* "I called you on [day] at [time] and [it rang out / went to voicemail / someone answered]. Is that typical?"
5. Which languages do callers use — Hindi, English, Punjabi, a mix?

## 3. Cost of the problem (5 min)

6. What do callers usually want — prices, stock, an order, delivery status, a booking?
7. When a call is missed, what usually happens to that customer?
8. Roughly what is an average order or booking worth to you? *(write the number down — it goes into the ROI report)*
9. If you got back even 10 of those missed calls a month, what would that mean for you?

## 4. What success looks like (4 min)

10. If we worked together, what would have to be true after 30 days for you to say "this was worth it"?
11. What's driving the timing — is this something you want fixed this month, or later in the year?
12. Who else should be part of this decision — a partner, family member, manager?
13. What matters most to you when choosing someone to help with this?

## 5. Show, don't tell (4 min)

> "Would it help if I shared how this works for a business like yours?"

- Play the demo agent (GTM-02) or a recorded call from the reference customer (with consent).
- Show one call summary and one order on the dashboard.
- Tie it back to their answers: "You said about [N] calls go unanswered after 7 pm — this is what those callers would hear."

## 6. Next step (3 min)

> "Would it help if we scheduled time to set this up on your number and test it with you?"

- Offer the plan that fits their call volume (≈ calls a day from the plan taglines).
- Mention the 7-day trial (outbound only) and, for paid plans, the setup included.
- Book the next meeting in the call. Send a summary within 2 hours: their problem in their words, the agreed success measure, the next date.

## Phrases to avoid → what to say instead

| Avoid | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| "Do you have a budget in mind?" | Makes it about cost, not value | "What outcomes matter most to you?" |
| "When can we close this?" | Pressure on timing, not fit | "What would success look like for you?" |
| "Let me tell you why we're the best." | Self-centred, salesy | "Would it help if I shared how we've helped a similar business?" |
| "Are you ready to buy today?" | Transactional | "What's your timeline for fixing this?" |
| "Who else are you talking to?" | Competitive | "What's most important to you when choosing a partner?" |
| "Just checking in." | No value | "I was thinking about your [after-hours calls] and had an idea. Want to hear it?" |
| "You really need this." | Presumptive | "Which challenges are you facing that we might help with?" |
| "Let me know if you have questions." | Puts the work on them | "Shall we book 20 minutes to go through it together?" |
| "This is a limited-time offer." | Artificial pressure | "What priorities are driving your timeline?" — use real limits only (e.g. the founding-client cap if D8 is approved) |
| "Can you introduce me to your boss?" | Goes around them | "Who else should be part of this conversation?" |

## Honesty rules

- Only promise what is built today (pricing page list). Integrations (Sheets, CRM, calendar, WhatsApp) are not available until S-IMPLEMENT.
- Use real numbers only: their own call volume and order value, or the reference customer's figures with consent.
- If the business gets fewer than ~2 calls a day and rarely misses any, say so and don't sell.
